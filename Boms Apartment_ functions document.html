Boms Apartment: functions document

This document lists every function the UI needs. The UI is built; none of these functions are connected yet. Each section matches a page in the sidebar.

1. Global functions (used on every page)

Function What it does
Login and logout Email and password sign-in, session token, auto logout after inactivity
Role-based access Roles: Worker, Manager, CEO/Admin. Hide pages and buttons the role cannot use
Notifications Bell icon: new booking, low stock, overdue invoice, new message
Global search Search guests, bookings, rooms by name, ID or status
Audit log Record who changed what and when (refunds, cancellations, price edits, stock changes)
Settings Profile, password, business details, tax rates, currency (NGN)
Export Download any table as PDF or Excel/CSV
Print Invoices, receipts, guest bills, reports

2. Dashboard

  • Summary cards: total bookings, check-ins today, check-outs today, revenue this month, each with change versus the previous period.
  • Revenue chart: revenue per day for a selectable range (this week, month, year).
  • Occupancy: occupied units divided by total units, shown as a percentage.
  • Recent bookings table: latest bookings with status. Clicking a row opens Guest Profile.

3. Reservation (guest profile and bookings)

Function Details
Create booking Guest, unit type, unit, dates, guests, requests, amenities. Check availability first
Availability check A unit cannot be double booked. Lock a unit briefly while a booking is being created
Price calculation nights x rate, plus extras, VAT (7.5%), service charge (5%). Discounts need Manager approval
Edit booking Change dates, unit, guests, notes. Recalculate the price and keep a history
Cancel and restore booking Apply the cancellation policy and refund rules. Record who cancelled and why
Check-in and check-out Change unit status, start and close the guest bill, trigger housekeeping
Guest profile Create and edit guest details, ID number, nationality, loyalty tier and points
Loyalty Earn points per stay, tier rules (e.g. Platinum), redeem points
Payments Cash, transfer, card, corporate invoice. Mark Paid, Part paid or Refunded
Booking history Filter by date range and search. Sort by any column
Invoice Generate, print and email the invoice for a booking

4. Rooms

  • List and filter: search, sort (popular, price) and filter by unit type.
  • Add and edit room type: name, size, bed type, guests, description, photos, price per night, features and facilities.
  • Availability counter: available units out of total units, per type.
  • Room status: Available, Occupied, Cleaning, Out of order. Updates automatically from bookings and housekeeping.
  • Seasonal pricing (optional): different rates for dates or weekends.

5. Messages

  • Conversation list with unread count and last message.
  • Send and receive messages in real time, with attachments.
  • Link a conversation to a guest and booking.
  • Mark as read, search conversations, assign to a staff member.
  • Optional: send by email, SMS or WhatsApp.

6. Housekeeping

  • Room status board: Clean, Dirty, In progress, Inspected.
  • Auto-create tasks: when a guest checks out, mark the room Dirty and create a cleaning task.
  • Assign tasks to staff, with priority and due time.
  • Update status from a phone. Record the time started and finished.
  • Supervisor inspection: approve a room before it becomes Available.
  • Report issues: link a maintenance request to a room.

7. Inventory

  • Items: name, category (Food, Linen, Supplies), unit, quantity, minimum level, cost, supplier.
  • Stock movements: add stock (restock), use stock, adjust (loss, damage). Record who and why.
  • Low stock alerts when quantity is at or below the minimum.
  • Purchase orders: create, approve and receive stock from suppliers. A received order updates quantity and records an expense.
  • Stock value: quantity x cost.
  • Link to restaurant (optional): recipes deduct ingredients automatically when a dish is sold.

8. Calendar

  • Monthly, weekly and daily views of bookings per unit.
  • Create or move a booking by clicking a date or dragging.
  • Colour by status (Confirmed, Checked in, Cancelled).
  • Block dates for maintenance.
  • Navigate months with Previous and Next.

9. Financials

  • Cards: revenue, expenses and net profit for a selectable period.
  • Revenue and expenses chart by day, week or month.
  • Transactions: every payment and expense with date, description, category, amount and status.
  • Record expense: category (salaries, power and fuel, repairs, internet, supplies), amount, note, receipt upload. Large amounts need Manager approval.
  • Invoices and receivables: list unpaid and overdue invoices, record payments, send reminders.
  • Reports: profit and loss, revenue by source (rooms, food, extras), occupancy, ADR and RevPAR.
    • Occupancy = rooms sold / rooms available
    • ADR = room revenue / rooms sold
    • RevPAR = room revenue / rooms available
  • Payroll (optional): staff list, salaries, deductions, payslips.

10. Reviews

  • Collect reviews: send a review request after check-out.
  • Statistics: positive and negative counts per day, plus an overall rating out of 5.
  • Category ratings: facilities, cleanliness, services, comfort, food and dining.
  • Reviews by country (from guest nationality).
  • Review list: sort by newest or rating, and reply to a review.

11. Concierge

  • Create a guest request (airport pickup, laundry, extra items, restaurant booking, taxi).
  • Assign to staff, set status (Open, Pending, Done) and track completion time.
  • Add the cost of a request to the guest bill.
  • Notify the guest when the request is completed.

12. Suggested data tables

users, roles, guests, room_types, rooms, bookings, booking_extras, payments, invoices, housekeeping_tasks, inventory_items, stock_movements, purchase_orders, suppliers, expenses, messages, reviews, concierge_requests, notifications, audit_logs.

Every table should include property_id, created_at, updated_at and created_by.

13. Business rules to confirm with the client

  1. Check-in and check-out times, and the late check-out charge.
  2. Cancellation and refund policy.
  3. Tax rates (VAT 7.5%, service charge 5%) and whether prices include tax.
  4. Discount limits per role.
  5. Deposit rules for long stays (weekly and monthly rates).
  6. Who approves expenses and above what amount.
  7. Minimum stock levels per item.
  8. How long to keep guest data (privacy and data protection law).

14. Suggested build order

  1. Login, roles, rooms and room types
  2. Bookings, guest profile, availability and price calculation
  3. Check-in and check-out, payments and invoices
  4. Housekeeping and calendar
  5. Inventory and expenses
  6. Financial reports and the dashboard
  7. Messages, reviews and concierge
